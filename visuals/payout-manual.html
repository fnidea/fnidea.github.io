Case study · Stripe reconciliation

The weekly reconciliation, by hand

Before any automation, every week went through four tools and a review by the CFO.

  1. Stripe website

    Download the reports

    Go to Reports and download the week's reports as CSV files. They're big, and the downloads are slow.

  2. Tableau

    Filter and transform

    The files are too big for Excel, so they go into Tableau. Moving each client's activity to its own timezone happens here.

  3. Tableau

    Export the results

    Each result goes back out of Tableau as its own separate report.

  4. Excel and Power Query

    Pull them together

    A consolidated workbook pulls every report in with Power Query and works out the net payout.

  5. Excel

    Check every tab

    Make sure the dates were filtered right and every tab pulled and updated. That's more than ten tabs.

  6. Excel

    Shape the entries

    Transform the week's data into the NetSuite import formats: invoices, the accrual entry, payments and the deposit.

  7. CFO

    Review and pull

    The CFO reviews the net payout, then pulls that amount from Stripe into our bank account.

  8. NetSuite

    Import and book

    Import the files and book the week, with hundreds of lines in each journal entry.

4 hrsa week, 6 at month end

From my own account of the process. The tab count is from memory, not counted from the file.
