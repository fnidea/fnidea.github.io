Case study · Stripe payout reconciliation

The weekly reconciliation, by hand

Before any automation, every week went through four tools before a journal entry could be posted.

  1. Stripe website

    Download the reports

    Go to Reports and download the week's reports as CSV files. They're big, and the downloads are slow.

  2. Tableau

    Filter and transform

    The files are too big for Excel, so they go into Tableau. Matching each client to its own timezone lives here, as a calculated field.

  3. Tableau

    Export the results

    Each result goes back out of Tableau as its own separate report.

  4. Excel and Power Query

    Pull them together

    A consolidated workbook pulls every report in with Power Query.

  5. Excel

    Check every tab

    Make sure the dates were filtered right and every tab pulled and updated. That's more than ten tabs, by my count.

  6. NetSuite

    Book it

    Create the journal entry and post it by hand.

4 hrsa week, 6 at month end

From my own account of the process. The tab count is my estimate, not counted from the file.
